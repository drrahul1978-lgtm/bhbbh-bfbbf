🧠 Talk to her

Eve cannot name an object she has never seen — that needs a model trained on a million photographs. What she can do is learn yours. Hold something up, name it a few times, and she will know it afterwards.

    🎓 Teach her this object

    Hold it in front of the camera and name it. Take about eight shots, moving it between each — different angles and distances teach her what matters and what does not. Same pose eight times teaches her very little.

    ⚠️ She recognises what you have taught her, in roughly the lighting you taught it in. She will confuse two objects of similar colour and shape — and will say so rather than guess. Anything she was never shown comes back as “I do not recognise that”, always. A recogniser that names its closest guess for something it has never seen is worse than useless.
