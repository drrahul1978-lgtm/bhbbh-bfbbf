    👩‍🏫 Teach her the right answer

    Disagree? Set the real grades and save it. Corrections are replayed in every future round, weighted four times heavier than practice cards — this is how Eve learns your cards and your camera.

    4️⃣ Talk to her — and watch her write code

    Eve understands a small set of requests through a second network trained on phrases (see intent.js). Ask her to connect to Home Assistant and she writes the adapter herself — real JavaScript, generated from the API's description, shown below before it runs. Honest limits: she recognises requests she has a skill for; she is not a chatbot and cannot program arbitrary things from a description.

    🔌 Connecting to Home Assistant

    The token stays in this browser and is never written into the code she generates. Browser note: your Home Assistant must allow this site under http.cors_allowed_origins — or skip CORS entirely by using node eve-connect.js on the Pi itself.
